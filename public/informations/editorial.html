Editorial Ethics Policy

The Journal of Clinical and Preventive Medicine is committed to maintaining the highest standards of publication ethics. The journal adheres to the principles of transparency, integrity, and accountability in all aspects of the publication process.

Responsibilities of Editors

  • Ensure the confidentiality of submitted manuscripts.
  • Make unbiased decisions based on the manuscript's academic merit.
  • Address ethical concerns, including plagiarism and conflicts of interest.

Responsibilities of Authors

  • Ensure the originality of their work and avoid plagiarism.
  • Disclose any conflicts of interest.
  • Provide accurate and complete references for all sources used.

Responsibilities of Reviewers

  • Provide constructive and unbiased feedback on submitted manuscripts.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of the review process.
  • Report any ethical concerns, such as plagiarism or duplicate submissions.

Plagiarism Policy

All submitted manuscripts are checked for plagiarism using reliable software. Manuscripts with significant plagiarism will be rejected immediately.

Conflict of Interest

Authors, reviewers, and editors must disclose any potential conflicts of interest that could influence the publication process.
