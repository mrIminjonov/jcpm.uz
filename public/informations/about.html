Journal of Clinical and Preventive Medicine

ISSN: 2181-3531

The Journal of Clinical and Preventive Medicine is a peer-reviewed, open-access international journal that publishes original research, reviews, and clinical case reports across a broad range of disciplines in modern medicine. The journal aims to advance scientific knowledge and support interdisciplinary collaboration in healthcare, clinical sciences, biomedical innovation, and public health policy.

Published quarterly (4 issues per year), the journal serves as a valuable platform for clinicians, researchers, educators, public health experts, and technology specialists.

🔎 Peer Review Policy

All submitted manuscripts undergo a double-blind peer review by subject matter experts and editorial board members. Reviewers evaluate submissions based on scientific rigor, originality, clarity, and ethical compliance. The journal maintains strict adherence to international publishing standards and the principles of academic integrity.
