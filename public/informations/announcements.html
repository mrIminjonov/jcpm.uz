Latest Announcements

📢 Call for Papers: Special Issue on Digital Health

We are pleased to announce a call for papers for a special issue on "Digital Health and Medical Technologies." Submissions are open until June 30, 2025. Authors are encouraged to submit original research, reviews, and case studies related to digital health innovations, AI in medicine, and telemedicine.

📅 Upcoming Conference: Clinical Medicine Innovations 2025

The Journal of Clinical and Preventive Medicine is proud to sponsor the "Clinical Medicine Innovations 2025" conference, which will be held on September 15-17, 2025, in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. Researchers and clinicians are invited to participate and present their work.

🎉 New Indexing Achievement

We are excited to share that the journal has been accepted for indexing in Scopus. This milestone reflects our commitment to publishing high-quality research and expanding our global reach.
